## Problem

Currently, the ScriptEditor component displays individual segment-level 'Narrate' buttons for segments without audio (lines 233-245 in ScriptEditor.tsx). This creates a redundant and confusing user experience because we already have a top-level 'Narrate' button in ChapterControls.tsx that handles narrating the entire chapter when no audio exists.

## Current Behavior
- Top-level 'Narrate' button in ChapterControls (lines 53-64) - narrates entire chapter
- Individual segment 'Narrate' buttons in ScriptEditor (lines 233-245) - redundant functionality

## Expected Behavior
- Only show the top-level 'Narrate' button for initial audio generation
- Individual segments should only show 'Regenerate' buttons after audio exists

## Solution
Remove the redundant segment-level 'Narrate' button block (lines 233-245) from ScriptEditor.tsx to maintain a clear and consistent user workflow.
